# Ethically Me, Anthony — A Personal Ethics Portfolio

A personal ethics portfolio by Thomas Anthony Dator for GED0061 - TE41, exploring my values, experiences, ethical decisions, and growth as a moral person.

**Live Website:** [https://tkmants.github.io/ethically-me/](https://tkmants.github.io/ethically-me/)

**Repository:** [https://github.com/tkmants/ethically-me](https://github.com/tkmants/ethically-me)

## About the Project

*Ethically Me, Anthony* is a personal portfolio that presents my understanding of ethics through real-life experiences and reflections. It explores how personal values and different ethical theories influence everyday decisions, relationships, responsibilities, and professional aspirations.

Rather than simply defining what is right or wrong, this portfolio reflects on the moral challenges I have encountered and the lessons I have learned from them.

## Portfolio Sections

The website is organized into four main sections:

### 1. Moral Self-Portrait
Introduces my personal ethics manifesto and three core values that guide my decisions and actions.

### 2. Moral Moments I
Presents six personal experiences involving everyday ethical dilemmas, including:

- Inspiration for a History Poster
- Helping a Friend Before His Exam
- The Gossip I Didn't Stop
- Shortcuts During Online Classes
- Losing My Temper Over a Game
- The Wrong Price at the Bakery

### 3. Moral Moments II
Introduces six additional experiences examined through different ethical theories:

- **Utilitarianism:** The Hours We Gave It
- **Deontology (Kantian Ethics):** The Answers Were Already There
- **Aristotle's Virtue Ethics:** The Projector I Forgot
- **Christian Ethics:** A Little Kindness After Midnight
- **Eastern Virtue Ethics:** A Candle for Those I Couldn't Visit
- **Distributive Justice:** Giving Credit Where It Belongs

Each moral moment follows a structured reflection covering the situation, ethical conflict, ethical lens, personal decision, and moral insight.

### 4. The Moral Person I Am Becoming
Concludes the portfolio with a personal reflection on ethical thinking, values, and ethical growth, followed by a personal commitment to practicing these principles in everyday life and in my future profession as a Computer Engineer.

## Running the Project Locally

This is a static website and does not require a backend or package installation.

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in a web browser.

Alternatively, use a local development server or the Live Server extension in Visual Studio Code.

## Deployment
